**Closer to Onshape: hover, selection and dragging**
- Sketches: hovering a point on a selected line, a line end after Esc, and a mirrored entity look as in Onshape; Dimension clears the selection; box select, Ctrl-window and the Select other key work as in Onshape.
- Assemblies: dragging a fixed part shows the ground symbol and Undo greys out while a part is dragged; selected parts, edges and vertices are outlined as in Onshape, also through the move triad.
- Drawings: hovering a view lights its box, lines and the sheet border light at Onshape's distances, a hovered centerline stays dashed, and a hovered table shows its grips.
- Section views keep their plane names and fills blue.

**Faster**
- Assemblies reopen from stored Part Studio results: the Isaac model in about 7 s instead of 36 s.
- Booleans that merge rounds and cylinders afterwards are several times faster.

**Fixed**
- External thread on a revolved cylinder, a convex round on a free-form face, and guided lofts follow Onshape's shape more closely.
- Saved Rip, Unfold and Refold features build again; a rolled bend keeps its sketch references.
- The app quits cleanly on logout, shutdown or kill, saving pending edits; on Mac it no longer ignores the quit signal after a recovered error.

**Closer to Onshape**
- Sheet metal: sketching on the flat pattern adds and removes material as in Onshape; the flat view fits as Onshape does.
- Drawings: sections through several parts hatch each part its own way; detail labels sit where Onshape places them.
- Feature Studio: selection, tab stops, comment and operator colours as in Onshape.
- Assembly configuration rows have Onshape's menu.
- Export: IGES is saved as .iges; STEP options laid out as in Onshape.
