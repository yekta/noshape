**Closer to Onshape**
- Sketch snaps: when one snap creates two constraints (for example perpendicular and coincident to a model edge), both icons show, placed as in Onshape. Any two snap zones that cross near the pointer now combine.
- Sketch Mirror works as in Onshape: a preselected line becomes the mirror line and the rest is mirrored at once, the tool stays on, model edges work as mirror lines, and each copy gets Onshape's mirror constraints. Prompts, field focus and the Create selection panel match Onshape's.
- Assembly Mirror dialog as in Onshape; mirrored copies are named "Part 2-Mirrored <1>".
- When mates can't all hold, only the mates in conflict turn red.
- The move triad stays a fixed size on screen; its ring menu has View normal to over a face.
- Sketch Use takes a circle's centre and the points of a mesh part, and stays linked when the sketch regenerates.
- Offset curve follows a circle round a cylinder or cone.
- Drawing exports (PDF, DXF, DWG) match Onshape's for broken-out sections, hole tables, angular and ordinate dimensions, break lines, callout borders, inspection bubbles, auxiliary and aligned section views and BOM tables. A broken-out section shows only in views projected after it.
- Release management: a version keeps its own part numbers, revisions and states; Revision history… is in tab and part menus after a release.

**Fixed**
- Dragging a part right after the pointer reaches it now moves it; a quick drag keeps its move.
- Several lofts, fillets, shells, part patterns (Remove and Intersect) and sheet-metal flats now build as in Onshape.

**Faster**
- The documents page redraws with every row selected in 13 ms (was 24 ms).
- Shell, Boolean and loft work on free-form faces is faster.
