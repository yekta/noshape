**Sketching**
- Constraint glyphs sit where Onshape puts them; hovering a sketch pattern lights its construction line.
- Hover stays on the point you're over when a dimension label is beside it, and an open right-click menu stops the hover under it.

**Part Studios**
- Faces that meet flat after a Boolean merge into one face, as in Onshape.

**Assemblies**
- Hover and selection with a mate dialog open, under a mate preview, and with hidden or transparent parts look as in Onshape; Alt no longer picks faded parts.
- Replicate numbers its copies outward from the seed, as in Onshape.

**Import and export**
- More Parasolid and IGES files import as in Onshape.
- Importing an STL or OBJ next to a STEP offers Units, as in Onshape.

**Faster**
- Chamfers and hose routing are back to their earlier speed (they had slowed in 0.4.69).
