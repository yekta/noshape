**Faster**
- Big assemblies: dragging a part in a large assembly stays smooth again, and a part with a ball joint keeps its orientation while you drag.
- Gear and herringbone models build several times faster; a swept screw thread cut takes milliseconds instead of a second.
- Fillets on models with many edge groups finish in a fraction of the time.

**Fixed**
- A revolve in some models no longer fails with "Error regenerating."
- Shell, fillet and sketch features in several saved documents work again after an edge-direction change was taken out.
- A part pattern placed in its own merge scope joins the parts it stands on, as in Onshape.
- Mirror and pattern of surfaces in some models no longer lose part of the body.
- A swept cut no longer leaves an invalid solid in screw models.
- Knurled rods and PEM nuts build as in Onshape.

**Closer to Onshape**
- Drawings: Datum target dialog, section view options, drawing chooser and table menus look and act as in Onshape; Compare shows no stray readout.
- Toolbar tooltips, hover and menus react as quickly as Onshape's.
- Sheet metal: sketches and extrudes keep their references in saved documents; flats of thickened parts lie as in Onshape.
- Assembly instance properties, mate preview colours and the Feature Studio editor's bracket highlight match Onshape.
- Version and workspace properties say Noshape.
