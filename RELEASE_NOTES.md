**Closer to Onshape**
- Release management as in Onshape's Professional plan: release reviews and comments, toasts, obsoletion, drafts ("Review Release", the drafts panel), releasing from inside a version, and a part number already pending in another release is refused with a "View release" link. Managed workflows start off for a new company, as in Onshape.
- Parts list › Where used… opens a panel listing the part's uses.
- Deviation analysis, Fill and variable-Fillet vertex rows, folders nested four deep, and the Material panel as in Onshape.
- Assembly dialogs: relations and patterns behave as Onshape's (Undo steps, Tab, units); a selected instance's outline shows through parts in front of it.
- Drawings: revision table menus and hole-table Collapse tags as in Onshape.
- A circle split into arcs extrudes with one side face, as Onshape does.

**Fixed**
- Bend relief on a partial Outer flange works again; corner breaks and flanges beside arcs build as in Onshape.
- Mate connectors on a cylinder's axis take Onshape's orientation.
- Several fillets and lofts match Onshape (variable fillets over part of an edge, full rounds over rounded ends, guided lofts).

**Faster**
- Sketch regeneration about twice as fast (expression-driven dimensions).
- The documents page selects 2,000 documents instantly (was 25 s); large version graphs, Variable Studios and Feature Studio files stay smooth.
- Sheet metal, face blends, fillets, Booleans and STL export faster.
