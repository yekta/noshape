**Sketching**
- Pierce slides a free circle or line onto the point without changing its size, and a line started on another line off its midpoint is perpendicular to it, as in Onshape.
- Mirror with a circle selected mirrors it when you click it; the sketch fillet radius you set is kept for the whole document.
- Dragging and hovering near dimension labels and the origin behaves as in Onshape.

**Part Studios**
- More fillets build as in Onshape: rounds taller than the face they roll on run past it, and rounds folding across a tighter one get Onshape's three-sided corner.
- A too-thick Shell points at its "Faces to remove" field; a Hole's boxes and focus behave as in Onshape.
- Faster: patterns of Booleans, some failing fillets and the impeller model regenerate quicker.

**Assemblies**
- Replicate lists its mates under itself; removing a mate connector is an undo step; a subassembly's menu has Where used…; the Measure panel and the move triad's hover look as in Onshape.

**Drawings**
- One Esc ends the dimension, ordinate, datum and surface finish tools, as in Onshape.
- Hole callouts, radius text, centerlines on circle quadrants, Geometric tolerance, foreshortened radii, the Tangent edges menu and the isometric view's Rotate view match Onshape.

**Fixed**
- If our own Boolean meets a shape it can't handle, it now falls back instead of failing.
