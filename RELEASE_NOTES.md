**Fixed**
- Windows: the update installer no longer force-closes other programs that have noshape loaded (a terminal, a backup tool); it closes only noshape's own copies and waits for its files.
- Assemblies: Linear, Rack and pinion and Screw relations move the follower the right way; a tapped Hole sized from standard content picks the fastener by its thread (M6x1 gives M6).
- Sweeps round a sharp corner of their path are mitred, as in Onshape.
- Fillets and chamfers on slanted hole rims that run off their face or into each other now build as in Onshape.

**Closer to Onshape**
- Frame: the Sketch profile library with search and a preview of each profile.
- Add custom features opens Onshape's panel with search.
- Assemblies: patterns and mate connectors can be configured; the linear pattern has Equal spacing, Centered and a third direction; the triad's rotation ring as in Onshape; folders and subassembly menus as in Onshape.
- Sketch: the conic and right-click menu behave as in Onshape's recordings.
- The agent API can now do everything the assembly dialogs do (measure, mass properties, replace, reorder, mate values, explode lines, properties).

**Faster**
- Cuts made by patterns of large tools are done one tool at a time where they don't overlap, much faster than before.
