**Sketch**
- Typing part of a variable's name in a value box suggests it as in Onshape: "fancontrol" or "fcb" finds #fan_control_board_depth (sketch dimensions, feature dialogs, configurations, the Variable Studio).
- A line's end clicked on a lit model edge lands on the edge and is coincident with it.
- A line that lights under the pointer is the one a click takes; hover reach matches Onshape's.
- A line is coloured by its own state: black when defined, with only a free end blue.
- A hovered curved face lights its silhouette edge.

**Documents and drawings**
- The documents page selects several documents (Ctrl toggles, Shift adds a range) and moves, trashes or restores them together; documents drag onto folders.
- Dragging a tab to the end of the tab bar scrolls it.
- Drawings: snapping reaches 12 px with Onshape's markers, a right-to-left box selects what it crosses, and pasted items land at the click.

**Modelling**
- Faster Shells and Booleans on shelled and patterned parts; sheet metal's coplanar merges run on Noshape's own Boolean.
- Chamfers along straight edges that run past their ends now build as in Onshape.
- Right-clicking a default plane in the graphics opens its menu (fixed in v0.4.47).
