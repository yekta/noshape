**Sketching**
- Geometry used (projected) from a part can be selected again: a click on a used line, arc or spline selects it, also in a sketch on a part's face, and shift-click and box select add them as in Onshape.
- Right-click › Select › Tangent connected edges follows a loop of used curves all the way round.
- A spline point placed level with the previous one gets Horizontal, as in Onshape.

**Part Studios**
- Picking a part's edge, face or sketch region reaches the way Onshape's does, and Select › leaves out the zero-length edges imported B-spline parts can have.
- Export of several parts is titled "Export 2 Parts" as in Onshape.
- More features build as in Onshape: a circular pattern about a hole's face, a Thin sweep along a part's straight edge, Full round where its side faces meet over the top, the knife-handle chamfer up a curving tip, and Booleans of imported sheet-metal parts.
- A Face pattern whose copies would only touch the part, and a Split by a tool that runs part way through, fail with Onshape's message instead of making broken geometry; a Rib that misses the part says "None of the ribs intersected a part."
- Documents saved where a Move face had failed open with their sketches still on the right faces.
- Faster: threaded parts (screws, nuts, standoffs) and some imported parts regenerate as fast as before or faster.

**Assemblies**
- Mate rotations about X or Y turn the way Onshape's do, and a Replicate or pattern dialog no longer re-evaluates the assembly while it is open.
- Assembly dialogs keep the keyboard where Onshape does, and mistakes show Onshape's errors.

**Fixed**
- Clicking a corner spline used from a derived part did nothing in a sketch.
