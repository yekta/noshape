User 11-14: every tool, dropdown and menu in live's drawing toolbar and panels, each marked in ours as matching,
differing or missing; the missing ones built in order of use (op, e2e test, ui-parity take). Generated by
`tools/ui-parity/drawing_matrix.py --run <a suite run's uip dir> --ours-menus <MENUDUMP json>` (this one: qa's 11-19 13:02 suite on main 291c74b0, its 6 drawing constraint / rectangle ▾ takes rerun on studios' 23:13 build after 6055ccb55 dropped those rows' keys, and our menus from drawing-ui's `drawing_context_menus_are_lives`); re-run it after a suite run and paste its output below the line.

Live's side: the toolbar dump (dom/toolbar-drawing.txt, Free plan 10-19, plus Edge condition symbol from the 11-01 map
and Revision table from the Pro-plan recordings), the right-click menu dumps (dom/context-menus-drawing*.txt, live2's
11-15 passes included) and the panel and dialog dumps (SURFACES in the script), and what live's recordings show.
Statuses:

- **=** live's recording shows it (the active tool, a panel text, a clicked menu row) and ours' state of that step
  shows it alike, or our menu of that entity has the row;
- **≠** a recording shows it and ours' state of that step doesn't;
- **shown** live's DOM dump has it in a panel and our build showed it in that panel (no take compares the two);
- **present** no recording shows it; it is in our sources (the dialog, field or row exists, not compared yet: a take
  is what's missing);
- **missing** neither; **accepted** a dump text live itself no longer shows (audit-drawing-dataexchange.md D3–D17);
  **n/a** cloud-only (Add comment).

**Result.** Nothing of live's is missing from ours: every toolbar tool and ▾ row, every right-click row (a submenu's
rows included) and every panel text is in ours. 1210 of 1874 live items (65%) match (11-17: 1046 of 1875); 446 more are
**shown**: live's DOM dump has them in a panel and our build showed them in that same panel (its fields, an open list
or menu, the tooltip; the run's own states, panel titles mapped in `OURS_TITLES`), with no take comparing the two
states. A panel dump's toolbar button or right-click row takes that item's status. 174 are present in our sources
only (no state of ours shows them yet): Create Drawing (47, screenshots checked separately), Move to sheet /
Replicate's page, and small rests. live2's 11-17 review dropped what full-page dumps carry besides their surface
(`PAGE_CHROME`, document names, a menu clicked earlier, display:none rows).

**Differing (6: 3 menu rows and the same rows caught in panel dumps), all dump conditions:** the surface finish on an edge's Flip and no Add leader as live's 10-28
recording (behaviours, qa request 1578; the 11-15 dump the other way, live2's retake newer than it); the ordinate's Reset
